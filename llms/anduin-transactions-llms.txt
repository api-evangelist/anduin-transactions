# Anduin Transactions

> Anduin builds digital fund operations software for private markets — venture capital, private equity, and hedge funds — streamlining investor onboarding, fund subscription, and fund data management. As of this profile the company publishes no public developer API; this file is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the company's public web surface.

## Products
- [Fund Subscription](https://anduintransact.com): Digital subscription document workflow for closing investors.
- [Data Room](https://anduintransact.com): Secure virtual data room for fund and deal documents.
- [Investor Data Management](https://anduintransact.com): Centralized investor and entity data.
- [Engagement Hub](https://anduintransact.com): Investor communications and engagement.
- [Integration Hub](https://anduintransact.com): Connectors into fund administration and downstream systems.
- [Data Extraction Service](https://anduintransact.com): Automated extraction of investor and subscription data.
- [Investor Portal / Investor Access](https://anduintransact.com): Investor-facing access to documents and status.
